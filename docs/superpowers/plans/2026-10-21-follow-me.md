# Follow-me Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Music follows a person from room to room, an empty room's lights and music switch themselves off, and a dark room lights up on arrival in the evening, all driven by the presence sensors the assistant router already publishes.

**Architecture:** A `lanbat-follow-me` plugin (like `plugins/android`) adds a module with typed settings under `lanbat.homeAssistant.followMe`. A pure Nix function (`lib/follow-me.nix`) turns those settings into Home Assistant helpers, template sensors and automations, which the module appends to `services.home-assistant.config`. A dry-run mode logs every decision to the logbook without acting.

**Tech Stack:** Nix modules, Home Assistant automations and templates, Music Assistant's HA integration (`music_assistant.transfer_queue`), the router's `sensor.lanbat_people_where_<key>`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-follow-me-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Defaults, verbatim from the spec: `arriveSeconds = 10`, `lightsOffMinutes = 5`, `followSeconds = 20`, `followCooldownSeconds = 60`, `musicPauseMinutes = 15`, `unlocatedMinutes = 20`, `darkLux = 30`, `sunBelow = -1`, `eveningFrom = "17:00"`, `nightFrom = "22:30"`, `morningTo = "06:30"`, `eveningBrightness = 100`, `nightBrightness = 20`, `dryRun = true`, `enable = false`.
- No entity id of a room, light, player or person appears in a `.nix` file under `modules/`, `lib/` or `plugins/`; they come from settings or from `lanbat.deployment`.
- Follow-me only ever calls `light.turn_on`/`light.turn_off` on entities listed in `rooms.<room>.lights`, and `media_player.media_pause`/`music_assistant.transfer_queue`/`media_player.media_stop` on Music Assistant players. Never a TV, lock, alarm, climate or camera entity.
- A room in `rooms` must be a room a presence sensor can report (a key of `lanbat.deployment.voiceRooms` or a `techo5Devices` room); evaluation fails naming the room otherwise.
- `unknown`/`unavailable` presence is never "empty": no action is taken.
- Follow `CONTRIBUTING.md` for the settings, example deploy files, docs and checks. Land the change on `origin/master` before deploying it.

## Review Focus

1. **A phone drops out for a minute** (BLE gap, adjacent rooms within 6 dB): the person is "home" or flips between two rooms. This is the normal pattern in the recorder (room, `home`, room within minutes). Expected: the room stays occupied for `unlocatedMinutes` after the sensor says `home`; nothing switches off before the timers (5/15 min); the music does not bounce (20 s stable, 60 s cooldown) and does not "follow" a person back into the room they never left.
2. **Two people in a room, one leaves:** lights and music stay; nothing moves.
3. **Guests and untracked people:** nobody tracked is in a room that is in use. Expected: "Guests here" suspends the lights-off and music-pause rules; music is only paused, never stopped, and lights follow-me did not turn on are never turned off.
4. **Someone switches a light off by hand and stays:** it stays off until they leave and arrive again.
5. **Router, MQTT or Home Assistant restarts:** the where sensors are `unknown`/`unavailable` for a while. Expected: no rule treats that as "empty", and no light turns on at startup.

### Task 3: Lights on when someone arrives in the dark

**Files:**
- Modify: `lib/follow-me.nix` (automation `followme_lights_on_<room_slug>` per room with lights)
- Test: `tests/follow-me.nix`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `act`, `sensor.followme_daypart`, `binary_sensor.followme_<room>_dark`, `input_boolean.followme_lit_<room>`.
- Produces: automation ids `followme_lights_on_<room_slug>`.

Trigger: `binary_sensor.followme_<room>_occupied` from `off` to `on`, `for` `arriveSeconds` (not a person's own sensor: a phone that drops out and returns does not arrive again, and a restart, `unavailable` to `on`, lights nothing). Conditions: not paused, the room dark, daypart `evening` or `night`, every light in the room off. Steps: `followme_lit_<room>` on, then `light.turn_on` with `brightness_pct` `eveningBrightness` (evening) or `nightBrightness` and a warm `color_temp_kelvin` (night).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests:** `lights-on-triggers-and-conditions` (trigger entities are exactly the tracked people's where sensors; conditions include dark, daypart and all-lights-off), `lights-on-night-is-dim-and-warm`, `lights-on-never-by-day`, `lights-on-skips-room-without-lights`.

### Task 5: Music follows a person

**Files:**
- Modify: `lib/follow-me.nix` (automations `followme_lastroom_<person_slug>` and `followme_music_follow`)
- Test: `tests/follow-me.nix`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `input_text.followme_lastroom_<person>`, `binary_sensor.followme_<room>_occupied`, the spike's result for the move action (Task 0 step 1).
- Produces: player lookup as the voice code does it: `integration_entities('music_assistant') | select('match', 'media_player\\.') | select('in', area_entities(<room>))`.

`followme_lastroom_<person>` writes the room name whenever that person's where sensor becomes a room follow-me covers (not `home`/`away`). `followme_music_follow` triggers on a where sensor `to` a covered room, `for` `followSeconds`; the source room is the person's last room before the change. Conditions: not paused; a source player is `playing`; the source room is now not occupied (the person was the only one there); the destination has a player that is not `playing`; the person has not moved music in the last `followCooldownSeconds` (last-triggered check). Steps (through `act`): `music_assistant.transfer_queue` targeting the destination player with `source_player` the source and `auto_play: true`. No separate stop: the spike found that Music Assistant clears the source queue, which stops it.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests:** `last-room-survives-home-gap`, `follow-needs-sole-occupant` (condition on the source room's occupied sensor), `follow-needs-free-destination`, `follow-cooldown`, `follow-needs-different-room` (the last room is not the destination), `follow-skips-rooms-with-music-off` (`music = false`), `follow-uses-only-music-assistant-players`.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run to confirm failure.** Step 3: implement. Step 4: run, expected PASS.
